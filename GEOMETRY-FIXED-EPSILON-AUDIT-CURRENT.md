## Exit criteria

- Every production occurrence has an owner and semantic classification.
- Geometric distance decisions use a shared `ToleranceContext` or document why they cannot.
- Parameter-space convergence thresholds are dimensionless and bounded by conditioning.
- Underflow guards and denominator regularizers are justified independently of drawing units.
- Test tolerances remain explicit and are not migrated as production decisions.
- No production occurrence remains `unclassified` or `geometric decision threshold — review required` without a documented disposition.
